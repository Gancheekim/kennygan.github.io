I am an incoming Ph.D student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Southern California (USC). I currently serve as a research assistant at Academia Sinica, working with Dr. Jun-Cheng chen on topics related on Generative Video Processing/Restoration & World Model. My research interests span from low-level (image/video signal processing, computer vision) to high-level (computational imaging, generative AI) vision tasks.
